# Thesis Format
Overview + Scientific article

### The thesis report consists of:
- A summary report covering goals, contex, process and process reflections
- A scientific article presenting the research context and questions, contributions, state-of-the-art, methods, experiments, results, discussion and conclusions.

As the studnets in most project will construct a software prototype including documentation the summary report is suggested to have a length of around 3-5 pages of approximately 2400 characters per student and the scientific article of a length of 5 pages per student in a standar ACM/IEEE template per student.
